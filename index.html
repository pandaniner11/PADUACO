Questions

Frequently asked.

What exactly is the Federal Retirement Review?

A 30–45 minute call with a Federal HR professional. We map your TSP, FEHB, FEGLI and tax picture against your retirement date — and flag the costly mistakes before they happen. No products are sold on the call.

Is this really free?

Yes. The review is free and there's no obligation. We make our living elsewhere — this is the work we'd want our own family to have access to, and it's how we meet most of the people we eventually help in deeper ways.

Do I have to be 55 or have $200K in my TSP?

Those are the markers for the full review call. If you're younger or earlier in your career, we'll still send you the Common Mistakes guide and the intro video — the moves you make in your 40s compound the most.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. We don't sell investments, annuities, or insurance on the call. If after the review you want help implementing something, we can point you to vetted, fee-only people — but that's your choice, not the pitch.

What should I bring to the call?

Your most recent SF-50 and TSP balance if you have them handy. If you don't — come anyway. We can still map the big picture from what you remember.

Who reviews me?

A Federal HR professional — someone who reads SF-50s and processes retirements for a living. Not an advisor, not a salesperson. Real HR.
