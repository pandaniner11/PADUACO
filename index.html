Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this really free?

The review costs you nothing and carries no obligation. Here is how it is paid for: if you go on to work with one of the companies we introduce you to, Padua Co receives compensation through a third party. Never from you. We want you to know that before you book, not after.

Is there a minimum age or TSP balance?

The full review call is built for people with roughly $75K or more in their TSP who are within reach of FERS retirement eligibility. If you are younger or earlier in your career, the free retirement checklist is still yours to download — the moves you make in your 40s compound the most.

Will you try to sell me something?

The review call is a review, not a sales appointment. If afterwards you want help putting something in place, we introduce you to a company we have vetted — and you should know Padua Co is compensated through a third party if you engage them. Read that company's own fees and disclosures before you decide. Walking away costs you nothing.

What should I bring to the call?

Your most recent SF-50 and TSP balance if you have them handy. If you don't — come anyway. We can still map the big picture from what you remember.

Who reviews me?

A specialist from a company Padua Co has vetted and trained. That specialist works for their own company, not for Padua Co. Padua Co's training is led by a former federal HR specialist with more than a decade of federal service.
