About Padua Co LLC

Padua Co LLC ("PaduaCo," "we," "us," or "our") operates paduaco.com and paduaco.us from Puerto Rico. We provide a free referral service connecting individuals seeking retirement-related information and services with participating companies.

Free Referral Service

PaduaCo does not charge consumers for its matching and referral service. PaduaCo is compensated in connection with these referrals. That compensation is paid to PaduaCo by a third party, not by you and not directly by the participating company, and PaduaCo also receives compensation for training the companies in its network. Because PaduaCo is paid when consumers engage participating companies, PaduaCo has a financial interest in those introductions. Participating companies are independent businesses and may charge for their own products or services. Their fees, terms, qualifications, and disclosures should be reviewed before you decide whether to engage them.

Your Information and Permission

We use the contact information and questionnaire responses you provide to respond to your request and arrange an introduction. We share that information with a participating company only after you approve the introduction to that company. We do not distribute your inquiry to additional participating companies without your permission.

Permission to share your information with one company does not authorize us to share it with other participating companies.

Once the approved company receives your information, its collection, use, retention, and disclosure of that information are subject to its own privacy policy and applicable law. Please review that company's privacy policy before approving an introduction.

Independent Companies

Participating companies are separate from PaduaCo and are responsible for their own communications, advice, recommendations, products, services, fees, and disclosures. An introduction is not a guarantee of a company's qualifications, suitability, services, or results.

No Government Affiliation

Padua Co LLC is a private business, not a government agency. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management, the Thrift Savings Plan, or any other federal agency. References to federal benefits or programs do not imply government approval or sponsorship.

No Professional Advice or Guaranteed Results

PaduaCo's website and referral service do not provide individualized investment, financial, legal, or tax advice. Submitting a request to PaduaCo does not establish a professional advisory relationship.

Website information and referrals do not constitute official benefit determinations or guarantees of retirement eligibility, benefit amounts, investment performance, or the ability to afford retirement. Verify official benefits information with your employing agency or the appropriate benefit administrator. Obtain advice from appropriately qualified professionals before making financial, legal, tax, or retirement decisions.

Protecting Sensitive Information

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, account passwords, complete financial account numbers, or unredacted personnel records through general inquiry forms. If a participating company needs sensitive documents, confirm its identity and secure submission procedures before providing them.

Communications

A request for an introduction is not blanket permission for unrelated companies to contact you. Any authorization for marketing calls or texts must be addressed separately where required. To stop communications from a participating company, contact that company directly or use the opt-out method included in its messages.

Website Use

Information on these websites is provided for general informational purposes and may not address your individual circumstances. You are responsible for providing accurate information and reviewing a participating company's terms before engaging its services.

Third-party websites and services have their own terms and privacy practices. Review those documents before submitting information or entering into an agreement.

Privacy Questions

For questions about information you submitted to PaduaCo, or to request access, correction, or deletion, use the contact method available on the website. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and any lawful retention obligations. Requests concerning information already received by an approved participating company should also be directed to that company.
